The **Settings** page has the options that are not about one site group. Every change is saved at once.

## Theme and language

Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark** for the theme. System follows your browser or operating system. The language is English or Dutch.

## Keyboard shortcuts

The page lists the two shortcuts with their current keys. **Change shortcuts** opens your browser's own shortcut settings, because only the browser can assign keys to an extension. Hiding and shortcuts explains what each shortcut does.

## Title prefix and history

The title prefix effect also appears in your browser history. The settings explain this next to the option, so you can decide before you turn it on.

## Diagnostics

**Copy diagnostics** puts technical details on your clipboard: versions, counts and whether site access is granted. Names, URL patterns and selectors are never included. Site addresses are included only when you tick **Include site addresses (origins)**. Paste the text in a bug report, as Troubleshooting suggests.
