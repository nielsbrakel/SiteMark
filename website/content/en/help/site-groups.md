A site group is one place to say which sites you mark and how. A group has a name, URL patterns, and one or more marks. Most people have a group per environment, such as “Production” and “Staging”.

## Switch a group on or off

Every group has an **On / Off** switch in the settings. A group that is off keeps all its patterns and marks, but draws nothing. In the popup, a disabled group shows up with a link to the settings. SiteMark also switches a group off by itself when you remove its last URL pattern, and tells you.

## Put groups in order

When several groups match one page, the group higher in the list wins the corner, the title prefix and the favicon. Use the arrows next to a group to move it up or down. Marks and effects explains how marks of several groups combine.

## Copy, export and delete

- **Duplicate site group** copies a group with its patterns and marks, a quick start for “Staging” after “Production”.
- **Export site group** saves just this group to a file. Import and export explains how to bring it back.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm. Right after, an **Undo** button brings the group back.

Deleting a group can leave access to a site that nothing uses any more. SiteMark offers to give that access back.
